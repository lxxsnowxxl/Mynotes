## Fonts

MyNotes no longer bundles the historical Google Sans font binaries that were
present in older project revisions. The application now resolves those legacy
font preference keys to Android's system sans-serif family, so no Google Sans
font binary is redistributed by MyNotes.

## Release checklist

Before a public release, generate the resolved Gradle dependency graph and
compare it with this notice so newly introduced transitive libraries and their
NOTICE requirements are not missed.

## Google Sans Flex

When the hidden developer font is unlocked and selected, MyNotes requests **Google Sans Flex** from the official Google Fonts provider at runtime. The legacy closed Google Sans binaries are not bundled. Google Sans Flex is licensed under the **SIL Open Font License 1.1**. A copy is included in `OFL-Google-Sans-Flex.txt` and `app/src/main/assets/legal/OFL-Google-Sans-Flex.txt`.
